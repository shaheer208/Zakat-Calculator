Zakat Manual & Guidelines

1. What is Zakat?

Zakat is one of the Five Pillars of Islam. It is a mandatory charitable contribution (2.5% of surplus wealth) intended to purify wealth and provide social security for the poor and needy.

2. The Nisab (Threshold)

Zakat is only obligatory if your net wealth meets or exceeds the Nisab. There are two standards for Nisab:

  • Gold Nisab: 7.5 Tola (approx. 87.48 grams) of 24K Gold.
  • Silver Nisab: 52.5 Tola (approx. 612.36 grams) of Silver.

Note: Most scholars recommend using the Silver Nisab for modern cash-based calculations, as it allows more people to contribute to the welfare of the poor.

3. Karat & Purity System

Jewelry is rarely pure 24K gold. To ensure fairness, this app uses a Karat correction factor:

  • 22K = 91.6% Pure
  • 21K = 87.5% Pure
  • 18K = 75.0% Pure
  • 14K = 58.3% Pure

If you have 10 Tola of 22K gold, the app calculates Zakat based on the value of 9.16 Tola of pure gold.

4. Zakatable vs. Non-Zakatable

Calculate These:
  • • Cash in hand or bank
  • • Gold and Silver (Jewelry/Coins)
  • • Stocks/Shares/Mutual Funds
  • • Property bought for resale
  • • Loans you are certain to get back
Do Not Calculate:
  • • Primary residence
  • • Personal car / Tools of trade
  • • Furniture / Clothing
  • • Stones like Diamonds/Pearls
  • • Property for rental income (only rent is zakatable)

5. Deductions (Debts)

You should subtract immediate debts (money you owe others) and upcoming business bills from your total. Do not subtract long-term mortgage/loan totals; only subtract the installments due right now or within the lunar year.
