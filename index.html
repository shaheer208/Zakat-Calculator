Zakat Guidelines

Nisab: The minimum amount of wealth a Muslim must possess before Zakat becomes obligatory.

  • Gold: 7.5 Tola / 87.48g
  • Silver: 52.5 Tola / 612.36g

Asset Types:

  • Cash in hand or bank.
  • Gold & Silver (Jewelry, coins, or bars).
  • Stocks, shares, and business merchandise.
  • Real estate or assets intended for resale.
  • Commercial livestock.
